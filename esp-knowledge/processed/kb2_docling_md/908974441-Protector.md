## Protector Functions

The Protector has four primary functions:

- Prevents entry of well fluid into the motor
- Provides pressure equalization to the motor
- Houses the thrust bearing to carry the downthrust developed by the pump
- Couples the torque developed in the

<!-- image -->

## Protector Chambers: Labyrinth

Labyrinth chamber relies on difference in SG between motor oil and well fluids

## Advantages

- Good in vertical wells
- Easily serviceable and re-usable
- Resistant to temperature and chemicals
- Inexpensive

## Disadvantages

- Not applicable to highly deviated wells
- Not applicable if oil SG &lt; 0.85SG (35 °API)

<!-- image -->

<!-- image -->

## Protector Chambers: Bag

A bag or positive seal provides a physical barrier that separates the clean oil from the contaminating well fluids .

## Advantages

- Positive  separation of well fluids from motor oil
- Operates in vertical and deviated wells
- Operates regardless of well fluids SG Disadvantages
- Rubber bags may be affected by temperature, solids and chemicals
- Re-usage is limited
