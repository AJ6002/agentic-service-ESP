    Total Dynamic Head (TDH) is a critical engineering term in the design and operation of Electric Submersible Pumps (ESP). It represents the total energy required to pump a specified volume of fluid from the well to the surface. TDH is calculated as the sum of three main components: Net Vertical Lift (NL), Tubing Friction Loss (TFL), and Tubing Head Pressure (THP). Tubing Friction Loss accounts for the energy lost due to friction in the tubing as the fluid flows through it. Tubing Head Pressure is the pressure the pump must overcome to push the fluid against the back pressure at the wellhead. The total dynamic head is essential for determining the pump's performance, motor requirements, and the number of stages needed to achieve the desired pumping capacity.
